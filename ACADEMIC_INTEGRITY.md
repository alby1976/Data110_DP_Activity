# Academic Integrity Notice

This repository contains an original academic project created by Albert Leung for DATA 110.

It is publicly available for portfolio, demonstration, transparency, and general learning. Public availability does not grant permission to submit this work, or a derivative of it, for academic credit.

## Permitted Activities

Subject to applicable law and institutional rules, students may:

- view the repository for general learning;
- link to or cite the repository where their instructor permits it;
- use the original City of Calgary dataset under its applicable licence;
- independently develop their own research questions, code, analysis, visualizations, and conclusions from public data;
- request written permission for another proposed use.

## Prohibited Activities

Without prior written permission, students may not:

- copy or lightly modify this project's code;
- reproduce its documentation or written analysis;
- submit its Power BI model, DAX measures, figures, dashboards, or presentation materials;
- translate, paraphrase, or reorganize substantial portions to disguise their source;
- present this work or a derivative of it as their own;
- remove or conceal copyright, attribution, or authorship notices;
- ask another person or an automated system to disguise copied material for submission.

## Independent Work and Citation

The City of Calgary dataset, public facts, the general research topic, and analytical ideas are not claimed as the author's exclusive property. Another student may independently study the same topic using the same public dataset.

Independent work means developing one's own code, classification rules, calculations, dashboard, interpretation, written analysis, and presentation. Merely changing variable names, colours, formatting, wording, or chart types does not make copied work independent.

Citation does not automatically make reuse acceptable. A student must also comply with the assignment instructions and the academic-integrity policies of their instructor and institution.

## Verification and Enforcement

The repository's Git history records the development and publication dates of the project. Instructors and educational institutions may compare suspected submissions with this repository when investigating potential academic misconduct.

Suspected copying may be reported to the relevant instructor or institution and may also constitute copyright infringement. Academic misconduct and copyright infringement are separate issues and may have different standards and remedies.

For permission requests, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
